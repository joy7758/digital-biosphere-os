# Stage 5.7 Limitations

1. The benchmark compares four deliberately different infrastructure profiles.
   It is a feature-selection experiment, not a neutral survey of named market
   products.
2. Candidate task probes are synthetic and deterministic. This run did not
   measure live remote Sandbox availability, enterprise workload behavior,
   production reliability, or real integration cost.
3. The frozen ROI score is an experimental comparison score. Monetary provider
   pricing was not assessed, so `compute_cost_status=NOT_ASSESSED`.
4. A positive score under the frozen formula is not financial ROI, commercial
   readiness, adoption, Certification, or production evidence.
5. `OPTION_D` was included in every selection, but seventeen of twenty-four
   sessions selected multiple candidates. Inclusion must not be restated as
   exclusive preference.
6. Two Ark sessions selected `OPTION_D` and `OPTION_B` but did not execute a
   useful candidate-task probe. They remain `task_completed=false`; their
   selections were not rewritten.
7. Exact model behavior may drift after the observed execution interval. Model
   identity is recorded, but provider-side implementation details are outside
   this evidence boundary.
8. The independent Auditor is deterministic code operating on retained
   evidence. It is not an Authority, standards body, or substitute for Human
   accountability.
9. One separate `qwen3.8-max-preview` advisory-review request timed out after
   120 seconds with no response and is retained as
   `POST_DISPATCH_UNKNOWN`. It is not counted among the 24 benchmark sessions.
10. The supplied DashScope model catalog and five direct K3 identifier probes
    did not expose K3. The Ark catalog also exposed K2-family identifiers but no
    K3 identifier at the time of inspection. Therefore:

```text
K3_AVAILABLE_ON_CONFIGURED_ENDPOINTS=false
K3_BENCHMARK_SESSION_COUNT=0
K3_QUALITY_ASSESSED=false
```

This is an endpoint-specific observation, not a claim that K3 is unavailable
from every provider or account.
