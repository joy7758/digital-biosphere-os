# Review Checklist

- [ ] scope and developer value are explicit
- [ ] exact sources and versions are cited
- [ ] object Owner and Authority are separate
- [ ] unknown, unsupported and failure states are preserved
- [ ] security, privacy and license were assessed
- [ ] positive and negative tests cover the claim
- [ ] AI assistance is disclosed
- [ ] no semantic elevation or capability overclaim exists
- [ ] required Human Decision is identified
