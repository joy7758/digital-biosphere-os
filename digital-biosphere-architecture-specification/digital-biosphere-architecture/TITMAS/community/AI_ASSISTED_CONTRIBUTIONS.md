# AI-assisted Contributions

AI coding, retrieval and review tools are welcome as disclosed assistance.

Every AI-assisted contribution must identify:

- model/tool and version when observable;
- human accountability reference;
- source material supplied to the tool;
- files and tests reviewed by a human or independent verifier;
- unsupported assumptions and generated content limitations.

An AI agent must not:

- self-assign Contributor, Reviewer or Maintainer roles;
- approve its own normative or security-sensitive work;
- fabricate citations, execution, tests, adoption or community members;
- expand scope, permission or external access;
- convert Recommendation into Decision;
- submit secrets or private data to a model.

The Human Owner remains responsible for consequential public, role, release and
authority decisions.
