## Purpose

This draft describes a possible, time-bounded evaluation of a TITMAS service
through approved black-box interfaces. It does not grant access, create a
customer, promise availability or disclose private core source.

## Candidate terms

- The Human or Organization is the contracting principal.
- An AI Agent may be an operational customer only within the principal's
  approved scope.
- Evaluation material remains confidential and may be used only for the
  recorded evaluation purpose.
- Access is revocable, expires unless renewed, and does not include repository
  history, internal tests, Provider credentials or real customer data.
- Results are evaluation evidence, not Truth, adoption or certification.

No legal entity, address, jurisdiction, legal opinion, price, customer,
service-level agreement or active obligation is asserted.
