## Accountability model

An AI Agent may consume a bounded service, but it is not the contracting or
final decision authority. The Human or Organization principal remains
responsible for authorized purpose, scope, input rights, review, external use
and consequences.

Agent output, execution evidence and verification do not independently create
Authority, Permission, Truth, certification or a contractual commitment.
Material exceptions must fail closed to Human Principal review.

No named customer, legal advice, jurisdiction or active agreement is created.
