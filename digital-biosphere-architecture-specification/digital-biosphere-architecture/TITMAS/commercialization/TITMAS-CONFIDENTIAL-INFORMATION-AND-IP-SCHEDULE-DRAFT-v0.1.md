## Candidate classification

Potential confidential material includes private core source, internal tests,
policy weights, private Git history, unpublished security findings, Provider
configuration, customer material and production evidence.

Potential public material must be reconstructed from an explicit allowlist and
must complete legal, security, commercial and license review. Prior public
exposure cannot be retracted and must remain recorded; it does not authorize
future export.

This schedule does not determine ownership, license rights, jurisdiction or
legal conclusions.
