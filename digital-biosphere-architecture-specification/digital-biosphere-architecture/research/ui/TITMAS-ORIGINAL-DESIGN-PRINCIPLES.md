# TITMAS Original Design Principles

Status: `DESIGN_BASELINE_CANDIDATE`

## 1. Evidence before decoration

Every operational page answers: who acted, under what bounded authority, what
was proposed, which policy/evidence revision was evaluated, what the result
was, what can be verified, and what remains unknown.

## 2. Mode is ambient context

`MONITOR` and `ENFORCE` are visible in the shell, page header and event detail.
Monitor always says `NO ACTIONS ARE BLOCKED`; Enforce always says `ACTIONS MAY
BE BLOCKED`. Color alone is never the carrier.

## 3. Machine truth and human explanation coexist

Identifiers, reason codes, schemas and digests remain copyable and exportable.
Chinese explanations are primary for commercial users; English technical terms
remain available without changing the canonical machine value.

## 4. High-impact actions slow down

Mode changes, key creation/revocation, approval, deployment and deletion use
explicit confirmation, scope, expiry and rollback context. Convenience never
creates authority.

## 5. Unknown is a first-class state

Empty evidence renders `NOT_ASSESSED`, never healthy. Verification failures,
missing keys and partial audit chains remain visible and exportable.

## 6. Dense but calm

Tables may be dense; hierarchy, whitespace, typography and fixed identifiers
keep them scannable. No cyberpunk spectacle, large particle field or animated
metaphor competes with evidence.

## 7. One platform, four public products

The console exposes Gate, Receipt, Audit and Health as coherent views over one
private platform. It does not duplicate identity, evidence, policy, receipt,
audit, tenant or key semantics.

## 8. Summer Sea remains the public Chinese brand

`夏日之海`, `titmas`, the TITMAS technical mark, the approved logo and current
redcrag.cn legal/filing footer remain intact. Commercial navigation does not
rewrite the brand contract.

## 9. Accessible by default

Keyboard paths, visible focus, readable tables, text+icon statuses, timezones,
language labels and reduced-motion behavior are part of acceptance.

## 10. Original, not look-alike

Research supplies interaction lessons only. TITMAS uses no third-party logo,
CSS, component code, screenshot, protected illustration, distinctive copy or
pixel-level page reconstruction.
