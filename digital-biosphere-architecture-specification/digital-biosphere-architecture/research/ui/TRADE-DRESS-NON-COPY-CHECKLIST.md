# Trade Dress Non-Copy Checklist

Status: `MANDATORY_BEFORE_PUBLIC_UI_MERGE`

- [ ] No third-party logo, icon set, illustration or screenshot is shipped.
- [ ] No third-party CSS, design token file or component source is copied.
- [ ] No page is a pixel-level reconstruction of a researched product.
- [ ] TITMAS does not reuse a competitor's distinctive color combination.
- [ ] Navigation names are generic functional terms or original TITMAS terms.
- [ ] Marketing copy is independently written and fact-checked.
- [ ] Receipt visuals do not resemble a legal seal, certificate or official badge.
- [ ] `夏日之海`, `titmas`, approved TITMAS marks and legal footer are preserved.
- [ ] Status is communicated with text and icon, not color alone.
- [ ] `MONITOR` explicitly states no blocking effect.
- [ ] `ENFORCE` explicitly states that actions may be blocked.
- [ ] Public claims distinguish research, candidate, pilot, deployed and production.
- [ ] Public UI exposes no private core, customer material, credential or attack corpus.
- [ ] Source URLs and access dates remain in the private research package only.
- [ ] A Human reviewer performs a final trade-dress and brand check before merge.
