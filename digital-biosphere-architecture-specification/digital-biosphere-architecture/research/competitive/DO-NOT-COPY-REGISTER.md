# Do Not Copy Register

TITMAS may learn category patterns but must not copy:

- third-party logos, illustrations, screenshots, CSS or component source;
- a vendor's distinctive color combination, navigation sequence or copy;
- Credo AI regulatory-intelligence packs;
- Cisco, WitnessAI, HiddenLayer or Baidu content-security classifiers;
- Permit or Cerbos policy languages and general authorization scope;
- Galileo, LangSmith or AgentLoop trace/evaluation platforms;
- cloud-provider model, MCP or A2A routing gateways;
- TraceSeal, GARL, Inntris, Duvera or AgentMint receipt schemas, brands,
  transparency implementations or public claims.

Every TITMAS design artifact must be source-attributed internally, implemented
from TITMAS contracts and checked for trade-dress independence before public
release.
