# Trust Center Comparison

## What a TITMAS trust surface may state

- exact public/private source boundary;
- supported deployment profiles;
- encryption and signing profiles;
- key expiry, rotation and revocation behavior;
- tenant isolation and deletion test evidence;
- incident, backup, restore and rollback procedures;
- dependency and SBOM evidence;
- known limitations and current maturity.

## What it may not state

- legal compliance certification;
- AI safety certification;
- production readiness before the production gate;
- customer adoption without customer evidence;
- receipt validity as truth, legality or authorization;
- a vendor security or compliance badge not actually held.

Official vendor pages show that enterprise buyers expect security documents,
deployment boundaries, retention, support and a sales path. They do not prove
that TITMAS currently meets those expectations.
