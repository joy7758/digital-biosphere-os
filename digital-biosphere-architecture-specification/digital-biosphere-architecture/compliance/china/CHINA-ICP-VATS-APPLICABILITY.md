## Current evidence

The public redcrag.cn footer displays `晋ICP备2026006409号-1` and a named
company. Display on a website is not authoritative verification of the filing,
legal entity, domain ownership, host location or licensed business scope.

```text
ICP_STATUS=DISPLAYED_NOT_AUTHORITY_VERIFIED
VATS_LICENSE_APPLICABILITY=HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED
PUBLIC_SELF_SERVICE_SALES=false
OFFLINE_B2B_CONTRACTED_PRIVATE_PILOT=AWAITING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW
```

Human review must classify the actual service: informational site, sale of the
operator's own software/service, online processing platform, third-party
information publication, transaction processing or another catalogue item.
`B2B SaaS` alone is not a legal classification.

Required evidence: MIIT filing query, entity/domain/hosting consistency,
business licence and scope, exact online functions, charging flow, contracting
flow, whether third parties publish information or transact, and counsel's
current Telecommunications Services Classification Catalogue analysis.
