# China AIGC Labeling Assessment

Status: `HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

Primary source: https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm

| Surface | Candidate status | Required behavior |
|---|---|---|
| deterministic Gate result and reason code | `LIKELY_NOT_GENERATIVE_CONTENT` | keep machine provenance and do not market as generated opinion |
| technical Receipt and Audit bundle | `LIKELY_NOT_GENERATIVE_CONTENT` | identify producer/version/signature; preserve technical limitations |
| static sales/documentation text drafted with AI assistance | `CONDITIONALLY_APPLICABLE` | Human editorial control; legal review of publication obligations |
| interactive public AI assistant | `LIKELY_APPLICABLE_IF_RELEASED` | explicit/implicit labeling, model disclosure and complaint/input controls review |
| private customer Agent output | `CONDITIONALLY_APPLICABLE` | customer/provider role and downstream publication determine obligations |

D29 does not require a public generative assistant. The commercial website and
console must remain usable without one. This classification is not a legal
conclusion.
