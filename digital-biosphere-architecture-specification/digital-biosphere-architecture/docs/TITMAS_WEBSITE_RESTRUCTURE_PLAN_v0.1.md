# TITMAS Website Restructure Plan v0.1

## C-01 homepage order

1. Discover what your Agents are doing and identify risk before critical
   actions cause external consequences.
2. Connect one test Agent or run a zero-customer-data synthetic demonstration.
3. Show `Agent`, `Action`, `Risk`, `Evidence Gap`, `Would Block`,
   `Would Need Approval`, `Receipt` and `Audit`.
4. Explain the progressive path from Observer to Monitor to Enforce.
5. Present exactly four product contracts: Gate, Receipt, Audit and Health.
6. Present bounded proof with evidence level and limitations.
7. Present design-partner and private-evaluation routes without adoption claims.

The first screen must not lead with Digital Biosphere theory, immune metaphors,
repository count, internal object names, a complex architecture diagram or raw
historical failure detail. Those facts remain discoverable under Trust Center,
Status, Known Limitations and Research.

## Target information architecture

```text
Platform | Products | Solutions | Proof | Access | Research | Company
Utilities: Trust Center | Status | Known Limitations | Agent entry
```

The Human home and machine entry must project the same four products, Observer
entry-layer classification, first-value paths, non-claims and current
availability. The website may not publish private core source, customer data,
attack material, keys or production configuration.

## Migration constraints

- Existing URLs remain until source, target, canonical, hreflang, sitemap and
  machine catalogs agree.
- A conflict stays visible and blocks redirect; 301/404 cannot hide it.
- `available_products` stays evidence-backed; four architecture slots do not
  become four available offerings.
- D04 failure, community status and `production_ready=false` remain reachable
  within two navigation steps.
- Visual or Logo changes require a separate authorization.
