# TITMAS Public Discovery and Proof Boundary v0.1

Status: `CLOSED_CORE_OPEN_DISCOVERY_AND_PROOF`

## Public surface

The bounded public surface may include product and solution facts,
machine-readable catalogs, limitations, public participation Schemas, synthetic
test vectors, Conformance feedback and allowlist-reconstructed proof bundles.

## Private surface

The following remain private: core implementation, policy and Gate engines,
Receipt and Audit engines, Health algorithms, private adapters, production
rules, bypass logic, internal attack materials, customer configuration,
deployment systems, credentials and private repository history.

```text
CLOSED_CORE=true
OPEN_DISCOVERY=true
OPEN_PROOF=true
OPEN_IMPLEMENTATION=false
PUBLIC_CORE_SOURCE=false
```

Public proof is a `CONTROLLED_SYNTHETIC_REFERENCE_PROOF`. It may establish that
a declared flow produced specific public outputs in a declared context. It is
not customer proof, production proof, independent external adoption, a formal
standard, certification or Truth.

Apache-2.0 may cover only reconstructed public files explicitly listed in a
public export manifest. It does not relicense any private or third-party asset.
