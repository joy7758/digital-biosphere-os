# TITMAS Health Snapshot / TITMAS 智能体健康快照

## Purpose

A bounded assessment for one software-delivery workflow and no more than three
Agent identities. It identifies structural gaps before any Monitor Pilot is
considered.

## Inputs

- public, synthetic, or explicitly authorized metadata only;
- identity, boundary, evidence, Gate, Receipt, and Audit references;
- no raw customer content by default;
- no credential, production secret, or Chain of Thought.

## Deliverables

- one coverage report using `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, `MISSING`, or
  `NOT_ASSESSED`;
- a limitations and missing-evidence register;
- a Monitor-Pilot suitability recommendation for Human review;
- an exit confirmation showing whether supplied material was retained.

## Non-effects

Health Snapshot creates no permission, authority, certification, credit score,
security rating, customer record, Workspace, production key, or execution
control. A recommendation is not acceptance into a Pilot.

## Acceptance

The Human contracting principal confirms that every declared input, finding,
unknown, and limitation is represented. Accuracy of scientific, business, or
legal conclusions is outside scope.
