# Security Questionnaire / 安全问卷

## Required review areas

- tenant isolation and workspace ownership;
- credential creation, scope, expiry, rotation and revocation;
- authentication and authorization failure behavior;
- Receipt integrity, verification and replay boundaries;
- audit export, retention and deletion;
- backup, restore and recovery evidence;
- model/provider data handling;
- incident contacts and notification;
- dependency, secret and vulnerability handling;
- rollback and emergency stop.

客户必须声明禁止的数据和动作。未关闭的 Critical/High finding、无法撤销的凭据或无法验证的隔离将阻止 Pilot。完成问卷不等于独立安全审计或安全认证。
