# FDO Vienna 60-Second Triple Script

## 60-Second Script

**English**  
This is not just an AI demo.

**中文**  
这不只是一个 AI 演示。

---

**English**  
It is a layered governance architecture for autonomous AI systems.

**中文**  
它是一套面向自治 AI 系统的分层治理架构。

---

**English**  
Our main argument is that autonomous systems need more than identity and metadata.

**中文**  
我们的核心观点是，自治系统需要的不只是身份和元数据。

---

**English**  
They also need structured interaction semantics, runtime governance, execution integrity, and exportable audit evidence.

**中文**  
它们还需要结构化的交互语义、运行治理、执行完整性，以及可导出的审计证据。

---

**English**  
So we organized the architecture into five layers.

**中文**  
所以我们把这套架构组织成五层。

---

**English**  
Persona object, interaction protocol, governance runtime, execution integrity, and audit evidence.

**中文**  
人格对象、交互协议、运行治理、执行完整性、审计证据。

---

**English**  
We are not claiming a finalized FDO standard.

**中文**  
我们并不是在声称这已经是一个最终定稿的 FDO 标准。

---

**English**  
We are identifying candidate FDO-facing entry artifacts.

**中文**  
我们是在识别面向 FDO 讨论的候选入口构件。

---

**English**  
The public route starts from the architecture hub, then branches to Agent Evidence, Verifiable Agent Demo, and ARO-Audit.

**中文**  
对外入口应先从 architecture hub 开始，再分流到 Agent Evidence、Verifiable Agent Demo 和 ARO-Audit。

---

**English**  
Our contribution is to move the discussion from generic AI capability toward governed, verifiable, and reviewable autonomous systems.

**中文**  
我们的贡献，是把讨论从泛化的 AI 能力，推进到可治理、可验证、可审阅的自治系统。
